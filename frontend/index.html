Admissions

Students can apply for admission through our online portal.

After submitting the application, the student's information will be reviewed by the teacher and then forwarded to the principal for final consideration.

Start Admission

How Admission Works

1. Student Registration

Students create an account and access the admission form.

2. Submit Application

Students enter their personal and admission details and submit the application.

3. Teacher Review

Authorized teachers review submitted student information and provide their recommendation.

4. Principal Decision

The authorized principal reviews the application and makes the final admission decision.
